## Overview

ECOSPHERE is a mobile operating surface for local businesses. Its product register is calm, capable and optimistic: it should feel like a well-kept local shop ledger, not a neon analytics console. The seller dashboard helps an owner understand the day, then move directly into orders, bookings or growth work.

## Colors

White surfaces sit on a soft mineral background. Forest green establishes authority, emerald marks action and success, lime and warm yellow are sparing indicators of opportunity. AI features use a quiet lavender surface so they are distinct without competing with business data. Never use navy glass overlays, opaque black panels, or white text on ordinary cards.

## Typography

Use Plus Jakarta Sans for screen titles and Inter for operational labels, data and body copy. Small uppercase labels can frame a section; headings remain sentence case and plain-spoken. Give money and primary metrics visual weight through size and spacing, not decorative effects.

## Layout

Use 16px outer gutters and 24px separation between major sections. The current business is named in the header; essential business signals follow immediately. A dashboard is a short route through the owner’s day, not a dense telemetry wall.

## Elevation & Depth

Surfaces are flat by default with a sage border. Interactive or floating surfaces may use a restrained soft shadow. The bottom navigation is the only persistent floating element; it should remain bright, warm and clearly separated from content.

## Shapes

Cards use 20px corners; controls use 14px; the bottom dock uses 26px. Avoid excessive pills except for compact statuses and the central AI action.

## Components

Cards, the seller switcher, AI insight surface and navigation dock all use the shared light surface and border language. Icons sit in subtle tinted containers, not dark glass. The primary AI action is emerald with a white icon; it is the screen’s single expressive focal point.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do make the owner’s next decision easy to scan. Do reserve stronger color for outcomes and calls to action. Do keep tap targets comfortable and labels legible. Don’t mix the former dark-glass system into the light theme. Don’t use “telemetry” language in owner-facing navigation; use business language such as activity, orders, earnings and growth.
